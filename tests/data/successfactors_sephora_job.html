Your Role at Sephora

As a Seasonal Associate, you'll be responsible for providing an outstanding client experience by supporting both store operations and onstage service during peak business moments. You'll create a welcoming experience while maintaining a stocked, visually ready store. If you thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment, this is your moment to Belong to Something Beautiful.

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver Exceptional Service: Support the team in creating outstanding client experiences and achieving sales goals.
  • Support Daily Operations: Manage inventory, stock replenishment, order fulfillment, and maintain operational standards.
  • Assist with Visual Merchandising: Ensure the store reflects Sephora's brand through accurate promotional setups and event execution.
  • Foster Inclusivity and Teamwork: Help promote a culture of openness where everyone feels empowered.
  • Uphold Sephora Standards: Follow company policies and help create a safe, secure, and enjoyable shopping experience.

Flexible Scheduling and Reliability

  • Weekly hours may vary, with an expected schedule of 0–14 hours per week based on business needs.
  • Must be available during peak retail operations including nights, weekends, and holidays.
  • Punctuality and consistent attendance are expected.
  • Available to be scheduled Monday, October 5 through Sunday, January 10.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Experience in a retail, hospitality, or client-focused environment preferred.
  • Excellent organizational and time management skills.
  • Quick learner with the ability to efficiently complete job tasks.
  • Strong commitment to client service.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Team player with a goal-driven mindset.
  • Comfortable in a fast-paced environment.
  • Ability to lift and carry up to 50 pounds.

Why You'll Love Working Here

  • Inclusion & Belonging: We pledge to create a beauty community where everyone's uniqueness is celebrated, respected, and honored.
  • Meaningful Rewards: Sephora offers comprehensive healthcare and wellbeing benefits based on eligibility.
